# Platform marketing boundary

Date: 2026-09-19
Owner: Beth Rudden

## Decision

The primary Bast Platform page is an outcome-led marketing page. It explains what Bast enables, why a person can trust the result, and how BastCare makes the platform tangible.

The primary journey should communicate:

- trusted organizational knowledge goes in;
- important outputs can show where they came from;
- people retain judgment and responsibility;
- Bast Agent OS helps a small team move faster around those decisions; and
- BastCare is the Platform and Agent OS in the palm of a patient or caregiver's hand.

## Public boundary

Do not lead the primary page with infrastructure mechanics, recovery-loop terminology, commit-history qualifications, maturity taxonomies, or migration details. Those remain available for technical buyers and diligence on the Architecture, Agent OS, and Evidence pages.

The marketing page should create understanding and curiosity. The deeper pages establish bounded technical credibility when a reader asks for it.
