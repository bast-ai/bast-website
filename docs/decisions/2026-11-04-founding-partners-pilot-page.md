## Decision

On 4 November 2026 Beth asked for `/pilot/` to be the founding-partners teaser of the BastCare Founding Partner Pilot deck. There is no separate teaser deck file. The page answers two questions: why this is different from the chart, and what the organization gets from analyzing transcripts stored in its vault.

The audience line is "Founding partners (hospitals, clinics and senior living)". The headline stays "Retain more of the conversation, so you can be present in the room." The primary action stays "Book a 30-minute call" (the existing Microsoft Bookings link), in the hero and at the end. The page names no price, seat count, week or month count, or CPT math.

The chart section leads with Beth Rudden's account of her mom and the pills, then three points: unasked questions, one relative carrying the visit, and the chart recording the care team's version.

The vault section states the flow. A resident, patient or caregiver opts in to share each visit; otherwise the transcript stays on the phone. Shared transcripts land in the organization's vault under a BAA. Bast analyzes them with permission and delivers the report. The report covers what people ask for most, where follow-up breaks, what families carry at home, and which needs the team can act on. Closer: "Your EHR tells you what was charted. The transcripts tell you what was said."

The consumer line "Audio is deleted once the summary is saved. Bast's servers don't save the transcript." is no longer the pilot page body. That account stays on `/bastcare/privacy/`, and the pilot page links there. Founding-partner privacy on this page is the vault flow above.

The header label "For care teams" is "Founding partners" on every page. The href stays `/pilot/`. The footer column is "Founding partners". The footer "Pilot one-pager (PDF)" is "Start here", linking to `/pilot/#book`. The one-pager PDF stays in the repo and is not the pilot call to action.

The Zarit measure and the Levoy 17% citation stay. Saved summaries in nine languages, including Filipino and Japanese, stay as the one line already published on the BastCare page.
