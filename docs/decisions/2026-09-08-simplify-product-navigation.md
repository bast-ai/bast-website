# Simplify the public navigation around Bast products

Date: 2026-09-08

## Decision

Reduce the public header to three choices: **Platform**, **BastCare**, and
**Contact**. Keep the homepage sections, legal pages, technical frames, company
information, and investor information accessible through contextual links and
the footer instead of presenting every destination in the primary navigation.

Keep the existing advisory pages and downloads intact, unlinked from public
product pages, excluded from the sitemap, and marked `noindex, nofollow`. Their
copy remains available to adapt for Beth Rudden's advisory and speaking
business rather than mixing a personal service offer into Bast's product site.

Give the Bast homepage a flatter, more editorial presentation inspired by
`bethrudden.ai`: quieter page color, restrained borders and shadows, a clearer
two-column hero, and more deliberate spacing. Retain Bast's self-hosted Estedad
typeface, logo, product imagery, and brand colors.

## Why

Bast's public site now needs to showcase the Bast platform and BastCare. The
previous header treated in-page sections, company material, product pages, and
advisory services as equal choices. That made the product story harder to scan
and created unnecessary inconsistency between pages.

Beth requested the product-first navigation and explicitly asked that the
advisory copy be preserved for her separate advisory and speaking business.

## Consequences

- The header is consistent across the public product, policy, support, and
  technical pages.
- Deep content remains available without competing with the main product paths.
- Advisory material is preserved verbatim and remains intentionally hidden from
  search and public navigation until it is adapted for the personal site.
- The visual refresh changes presentation, not public product claims.
