# Bast Platform public splash page

Date: 2026-09-01

## Decision

Publish a dedicated `/platform/` page and link it from the homepage Bast
Platform offering. The page reuses the platform positioning already public on
the homepage: approved knowledge sets the model boundary, the route to source
evidence remains inspectable, unsupported answers may be refused, and Bast can
be deployed hosted, in a private cloud, or on premises.

Beth requested the dedicated public page on 2026-09-01. The page introduces no
new performance numbers, customer claims, security certifications, or runtime
guarantees. It explains the relationship among infrastructure, data,
orchestration, application, and the answer/refusal path using the existing
public language.

## Visual relationship

The page uses the same calm, source-first design system as BastCare while
presenting Bast Platform as the governed system underneath an application. A
code-native system map shows approved knowledge entering the Bast control
layer and leaving as either a grounded answer with its source route or a clean
refusal.

## Homepage follow-up

Beth requested three refinements later on 2026-09-01:

- use the homepage Estedad title scale throughout the Platform page;
- give BastCare and Bast Platform equal full-width product panels; and
- place the Platform control-path diagram to the left of its homepage copy.

The Claim-Level Source Grounding promotion added by the earlier Noviant GEO
package was removed from the homepage at Beth's request. The detailed frame
remains public as a deeper technical resource but is no longer promoted from
the homepage.

The homepage Platform panel now presents two owner-supplied dashboard captures
from Bast's public YouTube demos in layered desktop product frames. The
interaction log leads because persistent, inspectable history of questions,
responses, routes, scores, timestamps, and flags is a core product capability;
the analytics view shows what that history makes possible. The dedicated
Platform page retains the control-path diagram. A primary-blue “Watch the
demos” action links directly to the homepage demo section.

Platform display headings use deliberate phrase-level line breaks so the page
reads in concise, controlled statements: “Build governed / AI on infrastructure
/ you control,” “Four layers / One inspectable route,” and “Your data / Your
decisions / Your operating boundary.” Their tracking now matches the shared
Estedad display treatment used by BastCare.
