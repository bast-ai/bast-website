# Simplify the advisory positioning

Date: 2026-08-21

## Decision

Lead the advisory page with a direct promise: **Make the right AI call.**

Describe the work in plain language for senior leaders. State what the buyer
gets, who they work with, and why the work is urgent. Keep the three existing
advisory paths, but name each problem in words a leader can repeat to a boss:

- use AI you can defend;
- meet regulatory duties that already apply; and
- help strong people do more with AI.

Move the foundational narrative to the end. It supports the offer; it no longer
has to explain the offer.

## Why

Advisor feedback on 2026-08-21 identified several phrases that required the
reader to interpret Bast's meaning before acting. The revised page uses fewer,
larger words; short statements; direct objects; and a simple three-step model.
It also replaces indirect scarcity language with the concrete limit of three
advisory clients at a time.

## Claims and boundaries

- The EU AI Act statement is limited to Article 50 transparency duties. The
  European Commission says those duties became enforceable on 2 August 2026.
- The stated fine ceiling, EUR 15 million or 3% of worldwide annual revenue,
  comes from Article 99 and is a maximum, not a promised or automatic penalty.
- The detail brief retains the exceptions, SME treatment, source links, and
  operational-not-legal-advice boundary.
- The user requested and approved this positioning direction by asking for the
  advisor's suggestions to be implemented.
