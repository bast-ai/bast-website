# BastCare 1.0.10 outcome story

Date: 2026-09-19
Owner: Beth Rudden

## Decision

Lead the public BastCare story with the caregiver and patient outcome: be present in the room, come back to what mattered, and include the family member who could not be there.

- Use **Bast Agent OS** publicly for the operating model behind stable products.
- Describe BastCare as the Bast Platform and Agent OS in the palm of your hand.
- Introduce **How This Summary Was Made** as a visible source check: the transcript is the source, and the product shows the words behind a key point and the matching moment when available.
- Reveal enough to create confidence and curiosity without publishing proprietary implementation details or model-internal reasoning.
- Say BastCare is free to download and that every feature is included for the first two recorded hours.
- Present 1.0.10 as “Now rolling out” while storefront propagation differs by country.
- Feature the verified Australian review separately from the automated U.S. review feed.

## Marketing boundary

The primary page is for downloads and subscriptions, not an engineering release report. Build cadence and test-count claims remain in internal evidence rather than the consumer journey. Technical architecture and delivery evidence stay available on deeper pages for readers who need it.

## Source boundary

“How This Summary Was Made” means user-visible provenance from a summary point to transcript words. It does not mean chain-of-thought, hidden reasoning, or disclosure of proprietary prompts and inference methods.
