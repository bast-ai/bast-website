Why this is different from the chart

“My mom told her doctor five times she didn’t want that many pills. It showed up nowhere in her chart.”

Beth Rudden

  • Unasked questions

    People leave the room and remember what they meant to say on the way home. The care team never hears it.

  • One relative carries it

    The most medically literate family member relays the visit to the others from memory and from the portal.

  • The chart records the care team’s version

    The record shows what was charted. It does not show what the resident, patient or caregiver said.

What you get from vault analysis

  1. Consent, every time

    A resident, patient or caregiver opts in to share each visit with your organization. Without that, the transcript stays on their phone.

  2. Your vault, under a BAA

    Shared transcripts land in your organization’s vault under a BAA. You own them.

  3. With your permission

    Bast analyzes the transcripts with your permission and delivers the report.

The report answers

  • What people ask for most

    Residents, patients and caregivers, in their own words.

  • Where follow-up breaks

    What people were told to do next, and what got in the way.

  • What families carry at home

    Pills, rides, cost, and who is doing the care.

  • Which needs your team can act on

    Needs that map to the words spoken in the visit.

Your EHR tells you what was charted. The transcripts tell you what was said.
