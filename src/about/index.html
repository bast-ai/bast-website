Bast's story.

Bast builds explainable healthcare AI. Data is a belonging of human experience. An answer should carry the source it came from, so a person can understand information in their own circumstances and knowledge can emerge.

Principles from nature.

Janine M. Benyus described how living systems endure. Bast uses those patterns in decisions about compute, architecture, data, and the relationship between people and machines.

Learn from nature as model, measure, and mentor.

Janine M. Benyus
Biomimicry: Innovation Inspired by Nature

  • Nature fits form to function. Use the right tool for the reason at hand.
  • Nature recycles everything. Reuse a verified answer before generating another.
  • Nature rewards cooperation. A person and a machine each bring what the other cannot.
  • Nature taps the power of limits. A trustworthy system knows what it cannot support.

Cynefin, and different ways of knowing.

Dave Snowden’s Cynefin framework sorts a situation by how cause and effect can be known. Each domain calls for a different way of knowing and acting.

  • Clear. Cause and effect are visible. Sense the facts, place them in a known category, and follow an established practice.
  • Complicated. Cause and effect take expertise to uncover. Sense, analyze, and respond. Several sound answers can exist.
  • Complex. Cause and effect show themselves in hindsight. Probe, sense what happens, and respond. The practice emerges from the probe.
  • Chaotic. Cause and effect cannot be found in time. Act to create stability, sense the effect, and respond.

Confusion, also called disorder, sits at the center. It is the state of not yet knowing which domain applies.

A way of acting that fits one domain does not fit another.

Bast’s principles page connects Bast’s approach to Dave Snowden’s insistence that action must fit the nature of the situation.
