Common questions

Try these first

Sign in is not working

Check your connection, try Sign in with Apple again, and confirm you are using the same Apple/Bast account. Do not create a second account to work around an error.

Recording will not start

Confirm everyone agreed, then check iPhone Settings for microphone and speech permissions. Return to BastCare and try again.

The summary is taking longer

Keep BastCare open and wait for the moving processing indicator. A slow summary does not mean your visit was lost. If a Try Again action appears, use it once the connection is stable.

Share or revoke is not updating

Use My Visits, open the visit, and review its written sharing status. Keep both phones online briefly. If the status still differs, send support the time and both app versions—never the summary.

An invitation will not open

Install or update BastCare, open the newest invitation link on the invited iPhone, and sign in with the intended account. Old or already-used invitations may not work.

I want to delete my account

Read the account-deletion page for the current deletion path and what it removes.
