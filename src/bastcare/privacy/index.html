In plain language

The short version

BastCare asks for consent before recording. You receive a plain-language summary. Audio stays on your iPhone until your summary is saved, then BastCare deletes it.

When you request a summary, BastCare masks configured names in a separate copy of your transcript and sends it securely through Bast to an AI provider for processing.

Bast’s servers do not save or log the transcript text used for this processing. Your saved transcript, summary, and available information about how the summary was made stay on your phone.

Translation also sends text for AI processing, with configured names masked. You can find the services we use in our AI providers list.

We do not sell your data.

Data we handle

BastCare handles the minimum information needed to provide an account, create a visit summary, deliver a share you request, and show whether those actions worked.

  • Account: your Bast identity, sign-in state, and device/account identifiers.
  • On your iPhone: temporary audio, the original transcript, your saved summary, available information about how the summary was made, sharing choices, and encryption keys.
  • AI processing: a separate copy of transcript text sent securely through Bast to the selected AI provider when you create or regenerate a summary, with configured names masked. Translation also sends text for processing with configured names masked.
  • Sharing: an encrypted copy of a summary only after you choose a CareTeam.
  • Technical records: content-free times, status, app version, delivery, consent, security, token-count, and deletion results.

How we protect your information

Audio stays on your iPhone until your summary is saved, then BastCare deletes it. If processing is unfinished, audio is kept for a 72-hour retry window and deleted when the app reopens after that window.

Bast’s servers do not save or log the transcript text used for this processing. Your saved transcript, summary, and available information about how the summary was made stay on your phone.

If you share a summary, Bast stores a locked copy so your chosen care team can receive it. This protection is called encryption. Your encryption keys stay on your device. Bast does not have the keys needed to unlock the stored sharing copy.

We also keep account information and technical records, such as whether a request succeeded.

Why we use data

We use information to provide features you request, secure accounts, create or regenerate summaries, deliver patient-directed shares, prevent abuse, diagnose content-free failures, and prove deletion. We do not sell your data. We do not use visit content for advertising. We do not train models on what you do in the app.

Sharing and your choices

Sharing nothing is always valid. Your saved transcript and summary stay on your iPhone. Separate copies are sent for AI processing as described above. You choose whether to export your transcript or share your summary. Before sharing, you can review the summary and select a CareTeam. After regeneration, share again if you want your CareTeam to receive the new summary. You can stop sharing later from My Visits.

  • Decline recording or cloud processing.
  • Keep every summary private.
  • Share with one CareTeam, change sharing, or revoke it.
  • View or download the original transcript.
  • Regenerate a summary, review the preview, and choose Use this summary only when you want to replace the current one.
  • Delete a visit or delete app data.

A transcript copy you download or send outside BastCare is controlled by the app, service, or person you choose. BastCare cannot recall that copy.

Retention and deletion

After a summary is saved successfully, BastCare deletes the visit audio. The original transcript and current summary stay protected on your iPhone until you delete the visit. Deleting a visit removes its transcript and summary; any exported copies remain outside BastCare’s control. Shared encrypted content remains available only while the share is active or until account deletion.

From Settings, you can remove BastCare data from one iPhone or delete your Bast account. Account deletion removes your Bast identity, active sessions, account-linked encrypted shares, CareTeam access, and the account’s local app data. Bast may retain only de-identified aggregate model token counts, a minimal content-free deletion event, and records required by law.

Security and service providers

BastCare uses authenticated HTTPS, account isolation, encrypted relay content, device-held keys, and least-privilege access. No internet service can promise perfect security. Bast has never had a data breach. If one ever affects you, we will tell you directly and quickly.

Apple provides device speech and Sign in with Apple. Bast also uses named infrastructure, database, identity, and model-processing providers only for the purposes described here.

BastCare can use Anthropic or OpenAI. We select the model for the task and may change that selection as quality, reliability, speed, or resource needs change. Each request goes only to the provider selected for that request.

Privacy Q&A

Your data. Your call.

Here's how BastCare handles your data, in plain words.

Do you sell my data?

No. We never sell your data or use it for ads. A few named providers help run BastCare, each with one job. See the provider list.

Do you train AI on my data?

No. We build AI that our customers own. We don't train models on what you do in the app.

How long do you keep it?

Audio is deleted once the summary is saved. Bast's servers don't save the transcript. Summaries belong to the user. They stay on your phone until you delete them. We keep only basic technical data that holds no visit content.

What happens when I delete my account?

Your account, your shared summaries, and your CareTeam access are removed from our servers, and BastCare is cleared from your phone. Delete your account anytime in Settings.

What can the app access?

Your microphone and speech recognition. BastCare asks for consent before recording. Your transcripts, summaries, and keys stay on your phone.

Who can read my visits?

You, and the people you choose. Shared summaries are locked with a key that lives on your phone, so even Bast can't open them.

What if Bast is acquired or closes?

Your visits stay private. Bast never holds anything readable from your visits, and no one, including any new owner, can open your summaries without your key.

Is health information protected?

Yes. Names are masked before a transcript is summarized, and our servers never log diagnoses or medications. Your health information belongs to you.

Can kids use BastCare?

Yes. BastCare is safe for kids. A child's doctor visit should include an adult.

How do I turn things off?

Right from your phone. Stop sharing a summary, delete a visit, or delete your account in Settings. Need help? Email community@bast.ai.

Have you had a breach?

No. Bast has never had a data breach. If one ever affects you, we'll tell you directly and quickly.

You are responsible for getting consent from every person present before you record. Laws differ by location. BastCare asks for consent before recording. That screen does not replace your responsibility to follow applicable recording and privacy law.

3. Not medical care

BastCare is not a medical device, medical advice, an emergency service, or a medical record system. It does not diagnose, treat, monitor, predict, or recommend care. Confirm health questions with a qualified professional. Do not use BastCare for emergencies.

4. Your account

Keep your sign-in and device secure. Do not share credentials or invitation secrets. Tell support promptly if you believe someone accessed your account without permission.

5. Your content and sharing

You keep your rights in content you provide. You permit Bast to process it only as needed to provide the feature you request. The original transcript stays on your iPhone until you delete the visit and can be used to regenerate its summary. Sharing is optional, scoped to your chosen CareTeam, and revocable. Do not use BastCare unlawfully; record without consent from the people present; impersonate someone; probe or disrupt the service; upload malicious material; violate another person’s rights; or use the app to provide automated clinical decisions.

7. Availability

Bast may maintain, change, suspend, or discontinue features. We will give notice when reasonably possible for material changes. BastCare is free to download.

8. Privacy and deletion

The BastCare Privacy Policy explains data handling. The account-deletion page explains how to delete an account and what deletion removes.

9. Contact and changes

Material changes receive a new version and effective date. Questions may be sent to community@bast.ai.

What this list means

A provider is not automatically given visit content.

Each provider receives only the information needed for its stated job. Ordinary open-source libraries that run inside the app or Bast services are not processors unless they send information to an outside company. We do not sell your data. We do not provide visit content for advertising.

BastCare chooses an AI model to suit the task. We evaluate quality, reliability, speed, and resource use, and may change the model or provider as those needs change. AI providers include Anthropic and OpenAI. A request uses the provider selected for that task; it does not automatically go to both.
