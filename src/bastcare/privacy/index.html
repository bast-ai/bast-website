BastCare privacy

Your visit stays yours.

BastCare is built so you choose when to record, what to keep, and who can see a summary.

Effective August 2, 2026Version 1.0

In 100 simple words

The short version

With everyone’s permission, record the conversation and receive a plain-language summary. Keep or download your transcript on your iPhone. Delete the visit to remove it. BastCare masks configured names before sending text securely to OpenAI for your summary. Bast does not save or log transcript text.

Data we handle

BastCare uses data to manage your account, create summaries, and share them when you ask.

  • Account: your Bast identity, sign-in state, and device/account identifiers.
  • On your iPhone: protected audio, your saved transcript and summary, sharing choices, and encryption keys.
  • Temporary processing: masked transcript text sent over authenticated HTTPS to OpenAI to create the summary you requested.
  • Sharing: an encrypted copy of a summary only after you choose a CareTeam.
  • Technical records: content-free times, status, app version, delivery, consent, security, token-count, and deletion results.

Audio and saved transcripts stay on your iPhone. Bast’s server does not save or log transcript text.

Why we use data

We use data to run BastCare, keep accounts secure, fix errors, and confirm deletion. We do not sell health information or use visit content for advertising.

Sharing and your choices

Your summary is private until you share it. Review it and choose a CareTeam. Stop sharing from My Visits.

  • Decline recording or cloud processing.
  • Keep every summary private.
  • Share with one CareTeam, change sharing, or revoke it.
  • Export a copy or delete app data.

Retention and deletion

Audio is deleted after your summary is saved. If processing fails, you can retry for 72 hours from the start of recording. After that, the audio is deleted the next time the app opens.

Delete a visit in My Visits to remove its transcript and summary. You can also remove a person from your CareTeam.

Regeneration is a temporary preview until you choose “Use this summary.” Share again to update your CareTeam. Deleting a visit or removing a CareTeam member ends their access when devices reconnect. Downloaded transcripts and PDFs cannot be recalled.

From Settings, you can remove BastCare data from one iPhone or delete your Bast account. Account deletion removes your Bast identity, active sessions, account-linked encrypted shares, CareTeam access, and the account’s local app data. Bast may retain only de-identified aggregate model token counts, a minimal content-free deletion event, and records required by law.

Security and service providers

BastCare uses authenticated HTTPS, account isolation, encrypted relay content, device-held keys, and least-privilege access. No internet service can promise perfect security.

Apple provides speech recognition and sign-in. Our provider list explains who else helps run BastCare and what they receive.

Children, regional rights, changes, and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, appeal, or delete personal information. Email us to make a privacy request. We may need to verify that the request concerns your account.

Material changes to vendors, purposes, retention, or visit-data flows require a new policy version and, where appropriate, a new choice in the app.

BastCare is not a medical device or medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat, monitor, predict, or recommend care. Do not use it for emergencies.
