BastCare terms and notices

Clear expectations for a tool you control.

These notices explain responsible recording, account security, CareTeam sharing, and the limits of BastCare.

Effective September 14, 2026Version 1.2

1. Using BastCare

BastCare helps organize and communicate information from a visit. Your use of the iOS app is also governed by Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. You may use BastCare only where permitted by law and these notices.

2. Permission to record

You are responsible for getting consent from every person present before you record. Laws differ by location. BastCare asks for consent before recording. That screen does not replace your responsibility to follow applicable recording and privacy law.

3. Not medical care

BastCare is not a medical device, medical advice, an emergency service, or a medical record system. It does not diagnose, treat, monitor, predict, or recommend care. Confirm health questions with a qualified professional. Do not use BastCare for emergencies.

4. Your account

Keep your sign-in and device secure. Do not share credentials or invitation secrets. Tell support promptly if you believe someone accessed your account without permission.

5. Your content and sharing

You keep your rights in content you provide. You permit Bast to process it only as needed to provide the feature you request. The original transcript stays on your iPhone until you delete the visit and can be used to regenerate its summary. Sharing is optional, scoped to your chosen CareTeam, and revocable. BastCare cannot recall transcripts or summaries you download, save, or send outside the app.

6. Acceptable use

Do not use BastCare unlawfully; record without consent from the people present; impersonate someone; probe or disrupt the service; upload malicious material; violate another person’s rights; or use the app to provide automated clinical decisions.

7. Availability

Bast may maintain, change, suspend, or discontinue features. We will give notice when reasonably possible for material changes. BastCare is free to download.

8. Privacy and deletion

The BastCare Privacy Policy explains data handling. The account-deletion page explains how to delete an account and what deletion removes.

9. Contact and changes

Material changes receive a new version and effective date. Questions may be sent to community@bast.ai.
