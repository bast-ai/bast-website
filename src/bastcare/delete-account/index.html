Your Bast account

Delete means delete the account - not just this phone.

The process is intentionally short: confirm once, Bast removes account-linked server data, then the iPhone clears its local copy.

In BastCare

Four clear steps

  1. 1

    Open Settings

    Choose Delete BastCare Account.

  2. 2

    Read and confirm

    BastCare explains the scope before anything changes.

  3. 3

    Bast deletes server data

    The authenticated request revokes sessions and removes account-linked data.

  4. 4

    This iPhone clears

    Only after server success, BastCare removes the account’s local app data.

Deleted

What full deletion covers

  • Bast identity and active sessions
  • Account-linked processing metadata
  • CareTeam memberships and relay access
  • Shared encrypted summary copies and keys linked to the account
  • Provider authorization where supported
  • All BastCare data for the account on this iPhone

May remain

Minimal content-free evidence

  • Aggregate or de-identified model token counts
  • A minimal deletion event proving the request completed
  • Any record the law specifically requires Bast to retain

These retained records must not contain identity, visit words, summaries, notes, credentials, or a link back to the deleted account.

Need help?

Use the support path.

If you cannot use the deletion action, email support from the address associated with your Bast account. Never email visit content or an Apple password.

We may ask for limited information to verify that the account belongs to you.
