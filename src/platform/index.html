The shift

Instead of asking the model to be honest, limit what it can say.

Bast sits between your knowledge and the model's output. Before an answer ships, it checks scope, pulls approved sources, and refuses when nothing supports a reply.

Supported question

Answers come from approved knowledge.

The model sees what passed the checks. Answers are written for the person asking, grounded in your own sources.

Unsupported question

A clean no is better than a confident guess.

If nothing supports an answer, Bast refuses before the model guesses.

Institutional trace

The route is inspectable.

See the sources, scores, and path behind an answer. People get clarity. You get accountability.
